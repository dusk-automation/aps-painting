How a job runs

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Look and quote

    Adam looks over the job with you and gives you a clear price for the work.

  2. Prep

    Surfaces are cleaned, sanded, filled and patched. Floors and furniture are covered.

  3. Paint

    The right paint for each surface, put on in proper coats and cut in clean.

  4. Walk-through

    You check the finish together before the job is called done.
