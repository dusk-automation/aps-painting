The painter

A painter who is fussy about the prep.

Adam has run A.P.S for more than ten years. His view of the trade is simple: put the right preparation in, use the right paint for the surface, and a paint job can change a home and add to what it is worth.

That is why the unglamorous part gets the time. Filling, sanding, patching and masking come first. The colour goes on last.

His own photos show the range: weatherboard cottages, sash windows, timber doors, hallways, ceilings and a church interior.

How Adam works

Three things that don't change.

  • Right preparation

    A finish only looks as good as what is under it. Surfaces are repaired and prepared before a tin is opened.

  • Right paint

    Timber, render, plaster and metal each need something different. Every surface gets the product that suits it.

  • Respect for your home

    Floors and furniture are covered and the work area is kept clean. Reviewers mention it too.
